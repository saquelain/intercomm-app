RideComm privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

RideComm is a group intercom for motorbike riders: an Android app and a web page for iPhone riders. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sold. This page explains what the app sends and where.

Your voice

While you're in a ride, your microphone audio is sent live to the other riders in the same ride through LiveKit (the voice service), encrypted in transit. It is not recorded or stored by RideComm.

Your name and photo

The name you type and the profile photo you pick are kept on your phone and shown to the riders in your rides.

Your location

  • Group map (off unless you switch it on): your position is shared with the riders in the ride while you're in it.
  • SOS: when you send an SOS, your position goes to the riders in the ride, and in a text message to the emergency numbers you chose.
  • Family can watch (off unless you switch it on): family members you send the watch link to can see your position and SOS during the ride.
  • Speed, distance and ride history use your GPS on your phone only. Ride history (routes, times, stops) is kept only on your phone and you can delete it in Settings.
  • Fuel & food finder: when you search, your position is sent to Google Maps (Google Places) or OpenStreetMap to find places near you.
  • Rain alerts (off unless you switch it on): every 15 minutes in a ride, a rough position (about 1 km) is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service.
  • Destination search sends what you type to OpenStreetMap's search.
  • Maps are shown by Google Maps or OpenStreetMap, which receive the area of the map being shown.

Messages in a ride

Votes, quick messages, hazards, regroup points, destinations, battery levels and similar ride messages go to the riders in the same ride through LiveKit and are not stored after the ride.

On your phone

Settings, emergency information, ride plans and ride history are stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app (or clearing the browser's data on iPhone) deletes them.

Services used

  • LiveKit (voice and ride messages)
  • Google Maps Platform (maps and places)
  • OpenStreetMap (maps, search, places)
  • Open-Meteo (weather, only with Rain alerts on)
  • GitHub Pages hosts the web pages.

Children

RideComm is meant for adult riders and isn't directed at children.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/saquelain/intercomm-app.
